# Experimental Investigation of Trailing Edge Noise from a NACA0018 Airfoil

## Overview
This project investigates **trailing edge noise generated by a NACA0018 airfoil** using acoustic measurements from a 2d microphone array. Trailing edge noise arises from the interaction between **turbulent boundary layer fluctuations and the airfoil trailing edge**, producing broadband aerodynamic noise.

The experiment aimed to measure the acoustic signature of the airfoil and analyse how the **flow velocity and boundary layer turbulence influence the generated noise**.

---

## Experimental Setup
Experiments were conducted in the **A tunnel at TU Delft** where a NACA0018 airfoil was exposed to uniform flow while acoustic measurements were recorded using a 2d microphone array positioned in the test section.

![](/images/naca0018_noise_setup.png)

The measurements focused on obtaining:

- sound pressure level (SPL)
- frequency spectra of the emitted noise
- variation of acoustic intensity with flow velocity

---
